3 · Connect goes to Cencori

The card's Connect opens Cencori's CostGraph page. There the customer clicks Connect, signs in to CostGraph, picks a tenant, and Cencori creates the push connection and sends the month so far. Nothing to fill in on CostGraph's side.

Flow

1. CostGraph → Integrations → Cencori → Connect
2. Opens console.cencori.com/settings/integrations/costgraph
3. Connect → Sign in with CostGraph (focus:write, integrations:read)
4. Choose the tenant (if more than one)
5. Cencori creates the connection and sends usage; nightly after that
