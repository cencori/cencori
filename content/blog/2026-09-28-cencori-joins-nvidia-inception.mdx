Cencori has joined the [NVIDIA Inception](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/startups/), supporting our cause to building AI infrastructure and accelerated computing.

We are pleased to join a community of companies working on the technology and systems that will shape how AI is built and operated.

Our mission is to **make the infrastructure required to build and run AI accessible to everyone, everywhere.** Joining NVIDIA Inception is a step in that work.

AI is becoming part of the systems the world operates. Applications and agents need reliable execution, persistent state, security, and visibility into their behavior. Enterprises need control over models, data, costs, and where their workloads run. As intelligence moves into laboratories, factories, networks, and machines, the computing underneath it has to meet the requirements of each environment.

That is the direction we are building Cencori toward.

The work ahead extends that foundation into more of the computing lifecycle: training, inference, deployment, and the infrastructure needed to operate AI across cloud, private, regional, and edge environments. This is a long-term undertaking, and access to the right technical resources and ecosystem matters as we develop it.

We look forward to exploring these resources as we build Cencori's computing capabilities and connect with other teams working on AI infrastructure.

For us, access to computing is also a question of who gets to build. A developer in Lagos, a researcher in Nairobi, or a small team beginning with an idea should have a path to the infrastructure their work requires. Our ambition is to support them from the first experiment through the systems they eventually operate.

**Built on Cencori. Run on Cencori.**

Explore [Cencori for developers](/developers) or read [our thesis](/thesis) to learn more about the computing infrastructure we are building.
