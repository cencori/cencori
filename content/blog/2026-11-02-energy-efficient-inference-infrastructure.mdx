Cencori is committing to build energy-efficient inference infrastructure, the computing foundation that AI models and agents run on.

Today, most businesses run AI on infrastructure they don't control, often far from their users and their data. That means higher costs, slower responses and data that leaves where it belongs. As AI moves into banking, payments, healthcare and government, where that data lives and who controls the infrastructure matters more and more.

Cencori is building the alternative across three layers:

**The platform.** This is where companies build, deploy and run AI agents, with models, memory, tools, security and monitoring in one place. Developers can deploy agents to a live address on Cencori.

**ENX inference.** This layer serves leading open models from infrastructure close to where customers operate, so they keep their data close and get faster responses.

**ENX silicon.** This is a long-term programme to design inference chips built for efficiency, to deliver more AI per watt and per dollar.

"Intelligence shouldn't be something you rent from somewhere else," said Bola Banjo, CEO of Cencori. "We're building the computing infrastructure AI runs on, efficiently, close to where it's used, and for the long term. This is a decade-long commitment, and we're starting now."

Cencori will share more on infrastructure partners, early customers and the ENX roadmap in the coming months.

## About Cencori

Cencori is a deep tech company building the computing infrastructure AI runs on, from the platform where AI agents are deployed to energy-efficient inference and silicon.
