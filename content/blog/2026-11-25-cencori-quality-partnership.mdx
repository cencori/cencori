Cencori is partnering with **[Quality](https://usequality.app)** to bring feedback from your live site into **[Tensor](https://www.tensorade.app)**, with everything the agent needs to fix what was reported.

Most bug reports reach a developer as a sentence and a screenshot. What the person clicked before it broke, the error in the console and the request that failed are all missing, so the first hour goes on reproducing the problem.

Quality records all of that when the feedback is left. Someone clicks any part of your site and writes a comment. Quality keeps the comment pinned to that place, with a replay of the session, the console logs and the network requests. With this partnership, a Tensor agent can read the same evidence and work from it.

## Fix a bug from the thread

Quality is in Tensor's extensions on the desktop app. Open **Settings → Extensions** and press **Sign in** beside Quality. There is no key to paste.

After that, give Tensor a Quality thread and ask it to fix it. The agent reads:

- **The thread.** What was asked for, who asked, and the discussion under it.
- **What the person did.** Their actions in order, which is usually how to reproduce the problem.
- **Console logs and network requests.** The error and its line, or the request that failed.
- **Screenshots.** What the page looked like at the moments that matter.

Then it finds the cause in your project and fixes that. It tells you which piece of evidence led it there, so you can check the reasoning before you accept the change.

The connection is read-only, and the agent sees only the Quality projects your account can open. It can't reply to a thread or resolve one. When the fix is done, you close the thread in Quality.

## Add Quality to an app you build in Tensor

On Tensor for the web, the agent knows how to set Quality up. Ask it to collect feedback from your users and it tells you where to find your Quality site ID, keeps the ID as a project variable and adds Quality's script to every page.

Quality ties a project to one address, so publish the app first. The feedback toolbar shows on the published app, not in the preview.

## We use it ourselves

Quality runs on cencori.com and on Tensor's own site. It's how we collect feedback on both.

## Get started

- **Connect:** in Tensor for desktop, **Settings → Extensions → Quality → Sign in**.
- **Get Tensor:** [tensorade.app](https://www.tensorade.app).
- **New to Quality?** Start at [usequality.app](https://usequality.app). It's free to start.
