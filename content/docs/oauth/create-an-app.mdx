An OAuth app is your product's identity on Cencori. People see its name when they are asked to approve access.

## Register the app

1. Open **Organization settings → OAuth apps**. You need to be an owner or admin of the organization.
2. Click **New app** and fill in:

| Field | Notes |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Name** | Shown as "Authorize *Name*". Up to 60 characters. It cannot contain "Cencori". |
| **Description** | Optional. A note for your own team; it is not shown to the people approving the app. Up to 200 characters. |
| **Website** | Optional. Must be `https://`. |
| **Redirect addresses** | Where Cencori sends people after they decide. 1 to 10. |
| **Permissions** | The most your app can ever ask for. See [Permissions](/docs/oauth/permissions). |

3. Click **Create app**. Cencori shows the **client ID** (`coa_…`) and **client secret** (`cos_…`).

The secret is shown once. Store it on your server, in the same place as your other credentials. Never ship it in a browser bundle, a mobile app or a public repository.

## Redirect addresses

A redirect address must be:

- `https://`, on any host and path, or
- `http://` on `localhost`, `127.0.0.1` or `[::1]`, for development.

It cannot contain a wildcard, a `#` fragment or a username and password. A query string is allowed.

Cencori compares the `redirect_uri` in a request with your list as text. `https://app.example.com/callback` and `https://app.example.com/callback/` are different addresses. Register each one you use, including the port for localhost.

## Verified apps

A new app is unverified. Its approval screen tells the person that Cencori has not verified who runs it. To have your app verified, email [support@cencori.com](mailto:support@cencori.com) with the client ID and your company details.

## Change or remove an app

In **Organization settings → OAuth apps**:

- **Edit** changes the name, description, website, redirect addresses and permissions. Removing a permission also removes it from everyone who already approved the app.
- **New secret** replaces the client secret. The old secret stops working at once, so your app cannot get or refresh tokens until you deploy the new one. Tokens already issued keep working.
- **Delete** removes the app. Everyone who approved it loses the connection and its tokens stop working at once. This cannot be undone.

An organization can register up to 25 apps.
