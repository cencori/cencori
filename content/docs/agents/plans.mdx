### Free trial

Sign up at [business.cencori.com](https://business.cencori.com). Every business starts with a free trial: **14 days or 50 conversations**, whichever comes first, with one agent. No card needed.

## What counts as a conversation

One customer's chat with your agent: on WhatsApp, on your website or through your share link. A conversation lasts up to 40 messages, or until there's been a day of quiet. After that, the customer's next message starts a new one.

Trying your agent in **Preview** doesn't count.

## When conversations run out

Chats already going keep going. New ones don't start:

- On **WhatsApp**, customers get a short reply asking them to call or visit you.
- On your **website**, the chat says it isn't taking new conversations.

The top bar shows how many you've used (for example **212 / 500 chats**), and the agent's overview warns you when you're out. Renew or upgrade under **Billing** to turn new conversations back on straight away.

## Paying

Open **Organization settings → Billing** (or click the chats counter in the top bar) and choose a plan. You pay in naira by card or bank transfer, for 30 days at a time.

- **Renewing early** keeps the days you had left: renew the same plan with 5 days to go and you get 35.
- **Changing plan** starts a new 30 days on the new plan straight away, with its full number of conversations.
