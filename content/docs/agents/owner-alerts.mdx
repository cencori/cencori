Owner alerts keep you in the loop without watching every chat. Open the agent's **Settings → Owner alerts**.

## Setup

- **Send alerts to:** up to 5 email addresses.
- **Time zone:** for the morning summary and the times in alerts.

## What you can get

| Alert | When |
| --- | --- |
| **Bookings** | An email for each booking, with the time, the details and who made it. |
| **Callback requests** | When someone needs a person, like a large party or a complaint. Needs **Request a callback** on, under Tools. |
| **Orders and payments** | When a customer places an order or pays. See [Payments and products](/docs/agents/payments-and-products). |
| **Morning summary** | Around 8am, the day before's bookings, orders and callbacks. Skipped on quiet days. |

**Latest alerts** at the bottom of the page lists what was sent recently.
