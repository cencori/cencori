An agent answers your customers for you: on your website, on a link you share, and on your WhatsApp number. You write what it should do, give it what it should know, switch on what it can do, and publish.

Everything here is done in the console under **Agents**. If you're building agents into your own product for your customers instead, see [Embedded Agents](/docs/embedded-agents/overview).

## Create an agent

Go to **Agents → New agent** and pick a starting point, or start blank. Starting points fill in a name, a description and instructions you can edit:

| Category | Starting points |
| --- | --- |
| Hospitality | Restaurant & lounge host, Hotel concierge |
| Support | Customer support, IT help desk |
| Sales | Inbound lead qualifier, Appointment setter |
| Commerce | Shopping assistant |
| Operations | Front desk receptionist, Data extractor |
| Healthcare | Clinic receptionist |
| Content | Research briefs, Onboarding guide |
| Education | Language practice tutor |

You can also ask [Copilot](/docs/agents/copilot) to build one for you.

### Restaurant & lounge host

This template asks for your venue's details up front (name, address, opening hours, phone, largest party, table time, how many bookings you take at once, time zone, deposit policy, menu and anything else guests ask about) and writes the agent from them. The agent's overview then shows a setup checklist:

1. **Add your menu.** A PDF or a pasted list both work.
2. **Connect Google Calendar.** Each reservation becomes an event you can see on your phone.
3. **Try it.** Ask about the menu and book a table in Preview, the way a guest would.
4. **Publish.** Guests always talk to the published version.
5. **Connect WhatsApp.** Guests message your WhatsApp number and the host replies.
6. **Get booking alerts.** An email for each booking and each guest who needs a call back.

## The agent's pages

| Page | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Activity, and the setup checklist for templates that have one. |
| **Agent** | Name, model and instructions. |
| **Knowledge** | Documents and websites it answers from. See [Knowledge](/docs/agents/knowledge). |
| **Tools** | What it can do: system tools, your webhooks, integrations and MCP servers. See [Tools](/docs/agents/tools). |
| **Runs** | Conversations and runs, with what the agent did in them. |
| **Customers** | The people it has talked to. |
| **Marketplace** | Publish the agent for others to use, or start from someone else's. |
| **Settings** | Website chat, WhatsApp and owner alerts. |

## Draft, Preview, Publish

Changes are saved to a draft. Use **Preview** to talk to the draft the way a customer would, tools included. When it's right, **Publish**: customers on every channel always talk to the published version, so you can keep editing without breaking anything live.

Agents know today's date and time, so "tomorrow at 7" means what the customer thinks it means.

## Where it talks

- **[Website](/docs/agents/website):** a chat on your site, added with one script tag, and a link you can share anywhere.
- **[WhatsApp](/docs/agents/whatsapp):** replies to messages, voice notes included, on your business number.

## Billing

Conversations are billed to your project's balance like any other model usage. Each channel has a daily conversation limit you set, so a spike in traffic can't drain your balance.
