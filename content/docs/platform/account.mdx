Your account is you, across every organization you belong to. Open it from your menu at the bottom of the sidebar: **Profile**, or **Settings** for everything.

## Profile

- **Avatar**, **first name**, **last name** and **username.**
- **Email** is shown but can't be changed here.

## Connected accounts

The sign-in providers linked to your account: **GitHub** and **Google**. Link both and you can sign in with either.

## Security

**Active sessions:** **Sign out other devices** ends every session except the one you're using. Do this if you signed in on a shared computer or lost a device.

Changing your password and two-factor authentication from the console are coming soon.

## Deleting your account

At the bottom of **Profile**, under **Danger zone**. Type the confirmation phrase to delete your account. This can't be undone.

If you own an organization your team still uses, talk to us before deleting your account so the team doesn't lose access.
