## What is an Organization?

An organization is the top-level container in Cencori. It contains:
- Multiple projects (your AI applications)
- Team members with different roles
- Billing and credit balance
- An audit log of administrative changes

## Organization Structure

```text
Organization (Acme Corp)
├── Projects
├── Production API
├── Staging API
└── Development
├── Team Members
├── Owner (Full access)
├── Admins (Manage projects, view billing)
└── Members (Access projects)
├── Billing
└── Audit Log
```

## Creating an Organization

When you first sign up to Cencori, a personal organization is automatically created for you. You can create additional organizations for different teams or companies.

### Steps to Create:
1. Open the user menu (your name, bottom-left of the sidebar)
2. Select **New organization**
3. Enter the organization name and create it

## User Roles

| Role | Permissions |
|------|-------------|
| **Owner** | Full organization control, including deleting the organization |
| **Admin** | Invite members and change their roles. Can view the audit log |
| **Member** | Access the organization's projects |

## Inviting Team Members

1. Open **Organization settings** (from the user menu)
2. Go to **Teams**
3. Click **Invite member**
4. Enter their email address
5. Select their role (Admin or Member)
6. Send the invitation

Inviting members requires a paid plan (Pro, Team, or Enterprise).

The invited user will receive an email with an invitation link. They must accept the invitation to join your organization.

## Switching Between Organizations

If you're a member of multiple organizations:
1. Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar
2. Pick the organization under **Organizations**
3. The console switches to that organization's projects

## Billing and Credits

Each organization has its own:
- Credit balance
- Billing history
- Payment methods
- Usage reports

Credits are not shared between organizations. If you create multiple organizations, you'll need to add credits to each one separately.

## Organization Settings

Open **Organization settings** from the user menu. The settings sidebar has:

### General
- Organization name
- Organization URL (slug)
- Organization ID

### Advanced
- Identity & access (SSO)
- Audit infrastructure
- Usage export
- Delete organization (owner only)

### Teams
- View members
- Invite members and change their roles
- Leave the organization

### Billing
- Credit balance and top-ups
- Plan and payment details

### Usage
- Usage across the organization's projects

### Audit Log
- Administrative changes across the organization. See [Audit Logs](/docs/platform/audit-logs).

## Best Practices

- Create separate organizations for completely different businesses or clients
- Use projects within an organization to separate environments (dev, staging, prod)
- Assign the minimum required role to team members (principle of least privilege)
- Regularly review team members and their roles
- Set up billing alerts to avoid unexpected charges
